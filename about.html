About Me

Hello! I'm Swayam Kamble, currently in my final year of B.Tech in Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) at St. Vincent Pallotti College of Engineering and Technology, Nagpur.

I'm passionate about integrating AI, ML, and IoT to create intelligent systems that solve practical problems through data-driven approaches.

I've worked on a variety of impactful projects including smart plugs, environmental monitoring systems, AI-based lab automation, and smart streetlight systems.

My work has earned recognition at events like the eLSI Hackathon (1st place out of 520+ colleges) and the College Startup Expo, where I received a ₹10,000 award for IoT innovation.

I'm an active member of the WiSE Lab and e-Yantra Lab, regularly conducting workshops and mentoring peers in embedded systems and emerging technologies.

Currently, I'm exploring Large Language Models, LangChain, and AI integration with embedded systems to push the boundaries of smart automation.
