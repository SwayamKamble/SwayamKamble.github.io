Privacy Policy

Thank you for visiting my personal portfolio website. I value your privacy and want to be transparent about how I collect, use, and protect your information.

Information Collection

When you visit my website, the following information may be collected automatically:

  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, referring pages, pages visited, time and date of visit.
  • Chat Interactions: Messages sent through the chat assistant are stored and processed using LangSmith (by LangChain) for response generation and analysis. These logs may be temporarily retained to improve chatbot accuracy.

When you use the contact form, I collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Subject of the message
  • Message content

Use of Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted via the contact form
  • Improve chatbot response quality and site experience
  • Analyze usage trends and prevent misuse
  • Ensure secure communication between client and server (using HTTPS)

Information Sharing

I do not share your personal contact data (name, email, message) with any third parties. The data is only used by me for contact and follow-up purposes.

However, non-personally identifiable data (e.g., site analytics) may be shared with external services to improve site performance.

Cookie Policy

Currently, no cookies are used on this website.

Third-Party Services

The following third-party tools may be used:

  • LangSmith: For storing and analyzing chatbot questions
  • Google Sheets: For storing contact form submissions
  • Google Fonts / Font Awesome: For visual styling

These tools may have their own data policies. Please refer to their respective privacy pages.

Your Data Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a summary of your personal data stored by this site
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to use your data at any time

Data Security

All data transfers occur over HTTPS. Reasonable security measures are taken to protect your data, although no system can guarantee 100% security.

Accuracy of Chatbot Responses

The responses provided by the chatbot are generated using AI and may not always be correct or accurate. They are intended for general informational purposes only and should not be solely relied upon for decision-making.
